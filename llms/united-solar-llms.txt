# United Solar

> United Solar (United Solar Polysilicon FZC SPC) is a clean-technology company producing ultra-high-purity polysilicon for the photovoltaic industry from its manufacturing facility in Oman's Sohar Free Zone — 100,000 metric tons annual capacity, supporting roughly 40 GW of solar module production. Backed by IDG Capital and the IFC. United Solar is an industrial manufacturer: it publishes no developer portal, no public APIs, and no machine-readable API artifacts.
